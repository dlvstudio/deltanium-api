Node registrations

A Store or Block Creator joins after paying the registration fee (bank transfer or PayPal with the NODE… content shown) and your approval. Approving signs the registration's signPayload with the Super Admin key in this browser; Central then spends the voucher and activates the node as soon as it answers on its endpoint. The private key never leaves this browser.

Match the bank / PayPal statement: amount and transfer content (NODE…) before you approve.
